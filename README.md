# Energy Share Fair: questionnaire mock-ups

Clickable mock-ups of the instruments planned for the ENERGY SHARE FAIR research project. They show what participants would see; they are not the final questionnaires.

**Live demo:** https://rennnas.github.io/energyfairshare_demos/

| Tab | Link | Who takes part | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Survey | [#survey](https://rennnas.github.io/energyfairshare_demos/#survey) | Non-members (public) | Example questions on household, home, appliances, finances, trust, fairness views and interest in energy communities |
| 2 · A/B test | [#abtest](https://rennnas.github.io/energyfairshare_demos/#abtest) | Non-members (public) | One of four annual statements (standard or itemised bill, with or without an app dashboard), then comprehension questions |
| 3 · Choice experiment | [#choice](https://rennnas.github.io/energyfairshare_demos/#choice) | Non-members (public) | Choices between two energy-sharing offers, or keeping the current contract; offers differ in cost, entry fee, registration, bill, operator, sharing rule, how community power is divided and term |
| 4 · Field test | [#field](https://rennnas.github.io/energyfairshare_demos/#field) | Members of the pilot communities | A member's real bill next to a simulated bill under a different allocation key (static, dynamic or hybrid), then questions on understanding, fairness, trust and staying |

In the field test, menus at the top switch between:

- **Use case:** UC1 building-level supply, UC2 regional energy sharing, UC3 utility-integrated community
- **Group:** test group, or frozen group that keeps the current system
- **Wave:** three waves of four months; each member sees each allocation key once, in random order

Each tab has a "What gets recorded" panel showing how answers would be stored.

## Notes

- All content, prices, communities and providers are fictitious.
- The screens are shortened. The real questionnaires will be longer, fielded in German and finalised after a pretest.
- The page is a single `index.html` with no build step and no data collection. Nothing you click is saved or sent anywhere.
