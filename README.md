# Energy Share Fair: questionnaire mock-ups

Clickable mock-ups of the three online instruments planned for the ENERGY SHARE FAIR research project. They show what participants would see; they are not the final questionnaires.

**Live demo:**

| Tab | Link | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Survey | `#survey` | Example questions on household, home, appliances, finances, trust and interest in energy communities |
| 2 · A/B test | `#abtest` | One of four annual statements (standard or itemised bill, with or without an app dashboard), then comprehension questions |
| 3 · Choice experiment | `#choice` | Choices between two energy-sharing offers, or keeping the current contract |

Each tab has a "What gets recorded" panel showing how answers would be stored.

## Notes

- All content, prices and the provider are fictitious.
- The screens are shortened. The real questionnaires will be longer, fielded in German and finalised after a pretest.
- The page is a single `index.html` with no build step and no data collection. Nothing you click is saved or sent anywhere.
